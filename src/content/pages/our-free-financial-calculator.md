Home equity represents the current market value of a home minus the outstanding balance on the mortgage. As homeowners pay down their mortgage, or as the home’s value increases, the equity—the part of the home truly “owned” by the homeowner—increases. Our Home Equity Calculator is a straightforward tool designed to provide a quick estimate of this equity. By entering the estimated home value and the remaining balance on the mortgage, users can gain an insight into the portion of their home they truly own. [Learn more](https://myhome.freddiemac.com/owning/equity-and-appreciation) about home equity.

Finally, we must also give some limitations about the calculator. While the Home Equity Calculator is a helpful instrument in financial planning, it’s important to recognize its limitations. This tool offers a simplified calculation that does not take into account other factors that could affect your total equity, such as second mortgages, home equity [loans](/mortgage-loan-calculator-online/), or lines of credit secured by your home. Additionally, the calculator assumes that the value you provide is an accurate assessment of your home’s current market value. Market values can fluctuate based on a variety of factors including market conditions, home improvements, or depreciation due to aging or damage. For a comprehensive understanding of your home equity and the financial opportunities it might provide, consulting a financial advisor or real estate professional is recommended.
