Running a newsletter contest, sweepstakes, raffle, or customer giveaway? You need a way to pick a winner that is fair, fast, and easy to explain to your entrants.

This **free random email picker** draws winners straight from your list of entrants. It runs entirely in your browser, so **we never see, upload, or store the emails you provide.**

## How to pick a random winner from a list of emails

1. **Get your entries.** Export them as a CSV from wherever you collected them: your email platform, a Google Form or Typeform, a spreadsheet, or your store's order list. You don't need to tidy the file first.
2. **Paste or upload the list.** Drop the CSV or TXT file onto the box above, or paste the emails in, one per line or separated by commas.
3. **Check the cleanup.** The tool trims spaces, removes blank lines and duplicate emails, and skips anything that isn't an email address, such as column headers or names. The results panel shows exactly how many entries are left.
4. **Choose winners and alternates.** Pick up to 50 winners, plus optional alternates in case a winner doesn't respond.
5. **Draw and save the results.** Copy the winners, or download a CSV with the draw date, time, and entry count for your records. Turn on masking before you share the results publicly.

## Tips for running a fair contest

- **Write the rules down first.** Say who can enter, when entries close, how many winners there will be, and when you'll draw.
- **Close entries before drawing.** Late entries added after the fact make any result look suspicious.
- **Decide how duplicates count.** This picker gives everyone one chance no matter how many times they entered. If extra entries are part of your rules (for referrals or daily entries), use the [weighted giveaway picker](/randomly-select-emails-for-giveaways/).
- **Use the first draw.** Rerunning until a result "feels right" isn't random. Draw alternates at the same time instead.
- **Keep a record.** Save the CSV, or screen-record the draw if you plan to share it.
- **Check the law.** Contests and sweepstakes have rules that vary by country and state, and email lists are personal data. Follow the rules that apply to you.
