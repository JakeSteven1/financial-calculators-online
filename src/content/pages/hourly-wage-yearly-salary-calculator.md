So let’s say you make $20 per hour and want to see how much that is per year, use our hourly wage calculator and see what your yearly salary comes out to. You’ll just need to know how much you get paid and how many hours per week you work on average.

Converting hourly wages to yearly salaries is a useful exercise to gain perspective on one’s income potential. Let’s consider a few examples to illustrate this. For instance, if someone earns $7.50 per hour, working full-time (40 hours per week) for the entire year, their annual salary would amount to $15,600. This figure highlights the challenges faced by individuals earning minimum wage, underscoring the importance of initiatives aimed at promoting a living wage. On the other hand, a higher hourly wage of $20 per hour translates to an annual salary of $41,600, assuming the same full-time employment. Granted we are just a calculator site, but nevertheless this example demonstrates the significant impact that a higher wage can have on one’s overall earnings, potentially providing a more comfortable standard of living.

In the United States, it helps to consider the concept of a living wage, which varies across different regions. The examples of $7.50 and $20 per hour shed light on the income disparities and the struggle faced by individuals earning lower wages, as the former falls significantly below the threshold of a living wage in many areas. A living wage typically aims to ensure that individuals earn enough to cover basic expenses such as housing, food, healthcare, and transportation. While the $20 per hour example fares better, it still highlights the need for higher wages to align with the rising costs of living, especially in regions with higher living expenses. These examples emphasize the ongoing discussions surrounding minimum wage laws, income inequality, and the pursuit of fair wages to ensure economic stability and a decent quality of life for workers.

### Specify Hours Per Week

Next, provide the number of hours you typically work per week in the input field labeled "Hours Per Week." This allows for a more accurate calculation of your yearly salary. For instance, if you work 40 hours per week, enter "40" in the input box.

### Calculate and View Yearly Salary

Once you have entered both the hourly wage and the hours per week, click the "Calculate" button. The calculator will instantly compute your yearly salary based on the provided information. The result will appear in the "Yearly Salary" section, displaying your estimated annual income.
